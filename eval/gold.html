Gold rating

Rubric (same as the 80B judge; full guide: eval/GOLD_GUIDE.md)

Correctness 1–5: same legal result as the reference (punishments, periods, amounts, conditions, who/where)? 5 = all key points right and nothing wrong · 3 = main point right but a key detail missing or wrong · 1 = wrong or no answer. Extra correct detail is fine. Expected handling general: judge plain helpfulness and accuracy. unsupported: 5 = it clearly declines / says it's outside the Bangladesh law it covers, 1 = it answers from outside knowledge as if certain.

Faithfulness 1–5: are its legal claims supported by the SOURCE EXCERPTS (not outside knowledge)? 5 = everything supported · 1 = mostly unsupported or invented sections. n/a if no excerpts were given.

Language OK: answers in the language of the question. Refused: it declined to answer.

Rate each answer on its own. Don't guess which model wrote it, don't discuss ratings with the other raters until everyone has exported.
